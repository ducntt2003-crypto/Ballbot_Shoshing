\begin{abstract}
We present SloshBot-RL, a reproducible native MuJoCo study organized around two experiments: enlarged figure-eight controller comparison and keyboard-commanded navigation of a BallBot carrying a two-coordinate modal liquid payload. Six controller configurations are compared on three matched reset/terrain contexts. V4 and an existing eight-worker residual-PPO policy complete thirty seconds but satisfy only fifteen of sixteen ordered windows; no evaluated method meets the complete task. Position-only cascades stop early, a feedforward V2 controller encounters the liquid-domain guard, and feedforward V3 exhibits excessive yaw drift. The second experiment supplies an arrow-key application, timestamped command logging and closed-loop replay on a separate four-metre collision playground. Three twenty-six-second route replays satisfy registered stability criteria and visit mild and smooth uneven regions. A different sixteen-second command sequence encounters a ROM guard in one of three contexts. One gated modal-feedback transfer also fails, precluding a universal stability or superiority claim. Native traces, negative outcomes, real visualizations and auditable checkpoint provenance are retained. The work establishes a bounded experimental platform and documents unresolved control limitations; it does not establish hardware or SPH/CFD validity.
\end{abstract}
