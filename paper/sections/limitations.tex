\section{Limitations and Future Work}
The liquid model retains a single lateral modal pair and assumed damping. It excludes higher modes, breaking waves, impacts, splashing, free-surface topology, and viscosity-dependent calibration. The mass distribution and approximate moment transfer are not a validated pressure-moment equivalence for a real tank. Equation consistency and bounded modal coordinates therefore cannot establish physical liquid accuracy. The fluid status remains \texttt{SPH\_BLOCKED}; neither a historical GPU demonstration nor a plotted blue marker changes that status.

The robot model is also a simulation representation. Capsule wheel geometry and anisotropic friction approximate omnidirectional contact. Procedural smooth heightfields have a narrow amplitude and wavelength distribution. Local exact predicates address represented geometric selection, whereas solver compliance, friction, slip, and effective actuator dynamics still require measurement. No hardware experiment, sensor-noise characterization, motor-delay identification, or sim-to-real transfer was performed in the evidence selected here.

Historical full-task completion evidence is available for particular V4 cohorts. The new parallel study adds full-duration residual-PPO trials, all of which still fail one tracking window. The known stress failure prevents a universal completion claim even for V4. The original sixteen-window contract is a synchronous time-window proxy and should not be mistaken for exact geometric node visitation. A future study could preregister a different spatial completion metric, but cannot apply it retrospectively to turn the archived failure into a pass.

Historical Y inference used two independent initialization seeds. The present campaign has only one training lineage, two development contexts, three held-out contexts, and disclosed I/O-recovery and seed-registration deviations. Multiple evaluation cells do not remove dependence on the same trained weights. Training repeatedly samples the beginning of the reference rather than the full trajectory phase distribution. No fresh final held-out set establishes the selected Y policy's benefit, and the exploratory mixture confounds marginal variance with multimodality. Confidence intervals for broad learning superiority would be misleading under this design.

Numerical support is local. Active-loop refinement is distinct from fixed-command replay; both are useful but answer different questions. The selected Y check preserves local effect signs, whereas some older startup comparisons show larger sensitivity. No full-horizon convergence campaign supports every reported controller ranking. Regression tests check software behavior and artifact relationships; their count is not an accuracy measure or an independent physical experiment.

Teacher search is computationally expensive and depends on full-state cloning. The sequential tested ownership contract does not imply safe concurrent callback operation. Finite backup tests and forecast tails do not certify recursive feasibility or hardware stopping behavior. Similarly, a supervisor's physically admissible degraded continuation may violate its old relative performance budget. Such cases retain explicit labels throughout the evidence package.

The highest-priority scientific extension is independent validation of the liquid ROM against measured or separately validated resolved-fluid response, including frequency, decay, force, and moment under translation and rotation. Wider terrain and initial-condition tests should then separate contact-domain validity from controller failure. Learning methods would need fresh full-horizon evaluation with matched information access, independent training replications, and a declared tracking--sloshing Pareto objective. Deployable control additionally requires causal liquid estimation or a verified robot-only backbone. These are targeted evidence gaps, not experiments performed or authorized by this writing phase.
