\section{Process-Isolated Parallel PPO Campaign}
The new campaign uses the unchanged privileged V4 backbone rather than the unsuccessful startup variant. A performance failure of the baseline's first tracking window does not preclude training when native physical and numerical guards remain valid. Eight spawned MuJoCo environments collect experience for one shared Standard PPO actor--critic. Each process owns its native data, controller memory, modal state, heightfield, random generator, clock, held action, and process-global contact callback. The existing serial-equivalence certificate is retained, and new phase-reset/replay checks cover the curriculum states. No controller gain, liquid damping, contact coefficient, or actuator authority is tuned in this campaign.

The observation retains the existing privileged $31$-feature contract. Both actor and V4 use true modal information; this is not a robot-only system. Actor and critic each use two $64$-unit Tanh layers. The raw diagonal-Gaussian action has initial log standard deviation $-2.3$. The unchanged mapping is $\delta=0.25\operatorname{clip}(a,-1,1)$, $u=\operatorname{clip}(u_{V4}+\delta,-1,1)$, and $\tau=-10\operatorname{clip}(u_b+0.03u,-1,1)$. The exponential tracking/balance/modal-energy/effort/action-variation reward and PPO hyperparameters follow the frozen implementation described above. Optimization uses up to $512$ policy intervals per worker, minibatches of $64$, up to five epochs, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, discount $0.99$, GAE $0.95$, clip $0.2$, zero entropy coefficient, value coefficient $0.5$, gradient cap $0.5$, and target KL $0.03$.

The enlarged reference is $x_d=0.75\sin(2\pi t/30)$ and $y_d=0.375\sin(4\pi t/30)$, with exact analytical derivatives. Native RK4 uses $2$ ms, and the actor updates every $10$ ms. The terrain remains the seeded $2$ mm maximum-absolute-height field, the modal bound remains $8.4$ mm, and motor limits remain $\pm10$ Nm. Final evaluation always starts from the canonical reset at reference clock zero and retains all sixteen synchronous tracking windows and the original safety, tracking, saturation, and path-length conditions.

\section{Registered Curriculum and Execution Accounting}
Level1 uses three-second startup episodes. Level2 uses contiguous five-second segments beginning at reference phases $0$, $3.75$, $7.5$, $15$, and $22.5$ s. Level3 uses fifteen-second segments at phases $0$, $7.5$, or $15$ s, and Level4 uses complete thirty-second episodes at phase zero. Advancing at the registered transition milestones depends on recent physical-duration completion rather than the baseline already solving all tracking windows. Training segments are not counted as full-task successes.

For nonzero-phase reset, the robot and sphere are placed at the corresponding desired planar location before integration, vertical support is adjusted to the actual heightfield facet, and controller/reference/history states are initialized at the explicit native clock. Velocities remain near rest. This is a curriculum initial-state distribution, not continuation from a canonical trajectory or a modification of the reference equations. Final tests do not use those translated curriculum starts.

The aggregate cap is $500,000$ executed environment transitions across all workers, with one independent policy seed, a conservative $1,200$ s training charge ceiling, $1,800$ s experimental-worker ceiling, and $8$ GiB RSS and disk limits. Milestones are $0$, $10,000$, $50,000$, $100,000$, $250,000$, and $500,000$. A shortened final rollout at a milestone avoids overshoot. Performance regressions do not automatically terminate the campaign; invalid physics, corrupted mappings, mathematical failures, or resource limits do.

Two implementation deviations are explicitly retained. An episode-archive I/O error occurred before the first optimizer update. The valid optimizer checkpoint was continued with reset environments, while the incomplete rollout was discarded; this is not exact native/RNG/rollout continuation. In addition, SB3 model setup overwrote the intended worker-reset seed list. The actual initial worker seeds were independently identified from archived initial states and disclosed. No final-held-out result was used to repair or relabel those deviations. The study is consequently exploratory rather than a claim of perfect preregistration adherence.
