\begin{abstract}
SloshBot-RL provides two native MuJoCo experiments for a BallBot carrying a two-coordinate modal liquid payload: enlarged figure-eight controller comparison and keyboard-commanded terrain traversal. The preserved figure-eight benchmark remains unresolved; privileged V4 and an existing residual PPO complete thirty seconds but only fifteen of sixteen windows. A versioned terrain study evaluates eighteen twenty-six-second routes on six normalized collision heightfields. Seventeen satisfy the registered geometric/physical route conditions, while fifteen also satisfy inherited velocity/yaw-quality thresholds. One connected-course trial reaches the ROM guard. Actual sphere-centre measurements show ascent and descent over $1.8$ mm features, but inherited compliant penetration exceeds the feature height and actuator saturation is substantial. A read-only Bessel surface reconstruction, normalized by the modal liquid first moment, supplies interactive visualization without altering physics. Independent Chrono GPU screening adds $1600$ isolated-container SPH steps; finer cases fail pressure/force screens and mass differs across resolutions, preventing dynamic ROM validation. The release contributes reproducible measurements, native visualization and explicit negative evidence, not a claim of validated real-fluid, hardware or broadly robust control performance.
\end{abstract}
