\section{Conclusion}
The two-experiment release preserves unresolved figure-eight outcomes while adding measured low-terrain traversal and a state-driven liquid visualization. Seventeen of eighteen routes meet geometric/physical criteria, but motion-quality failures, limited motor headroom and contact penetration larger than the features bound the result. The surface is a derived linear visualization, and independent SPH screening does not pass the full numerical gate or establish dynamic ROM accuracy. The liquid status remains \texttt{SPH\_BLOCKED}. Stronger claims require contact/compliance calibration, actuator-headroom assessment and fixed-geometry fluid validation before larger obstacles, new training or full fluid--robot co-simulation. No such additional campaign is launched here.
